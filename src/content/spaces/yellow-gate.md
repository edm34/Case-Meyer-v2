From the street the house is a single low wall. A thin concrete lintel runs its full length and carries a hedge of vines, bougainvillea and trumpet flowers that has grown over the wall for decades. Beneath the lintel there are only two openings: a narrow white door for people, and the wide yellow gate for cars.

The gate is the one note of colour on the street. It slides, rather than swings, so the wall is never broken. The yellow is the same warm chrome yellow Barragán used in the corridor of Casa Gilardi two years earlier.
