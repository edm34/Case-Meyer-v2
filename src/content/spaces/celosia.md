A celosía is a lattice: a screen that lets light and air through while keeping the inside private. At Casa Meyer the lattice is painted a deep bougainvillea pink, and one of its openings is filled with a single square of bright green.

Barragán's study for the screen gives its measurements in centimetres, 299 wide by 277 high, with a door set into the right third. The green square sits beside the door, where a hand reaches for it. The lattice is the house's signature, and the starting point for the design of this site.
